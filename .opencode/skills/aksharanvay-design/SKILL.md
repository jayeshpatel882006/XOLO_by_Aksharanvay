# AKSHARANVAY Design Philosophy

Create original, memorable digital experiences.

Principles:
- Premium and playful interfaces.
- Tactile interactions.
- Expressive animations.
- Excellent typography.
- Thoughtful visual hierarchy.
- Original visual identity.
- Delightful micro-interactions.
- Performance-conscious implementation.

Never create generic template interfaces.
Never copy another brand's identity.
Keep branding configurable until official
brand guidelines are finalized.

Prioritize accessibility and performance.
